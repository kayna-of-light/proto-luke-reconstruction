**Edition:** K. Holl, *Epiphanius II* (GCS 31; Leipzig: Hinrichs, 1922), pp. 107–117. Archive.org identifier `epiphanius02epip`; PDF page = Holl page + 12.

**Transcribed from the page images** by the auditing agent on 2026-10-12. Accents follow Holl. «» marks Holl's quotation marks (Epiphanius quoting Marcion's text). Luke references are Holl's source apparatus. The refutations (ἔλεγχοι, 42.11.17) on pp. 118–183 repeat each scholion with comment. They are consulted verse by verse and cited in the apparatus where they add anything.

**How to read the formulae**
- Epiphanius' verbs of removal are **reports of content**: the passage is not in Marcion's text. They are recorded as `A−`. His attribution of motive ("he cut") is polemic and is not used.
  - παρέκοψε, ἀπέκοψε, περιέκοψε: "he cut off"
  - οὐκ εἶχε / οὐκ ἔχει: "he did not have"
- The verbs of alteration are **reports of a variant reading** in Marcion's text. Neither establishes which reading is older.
  - ἀντὶ τοῦ X εἶχε Y / ἀντὶ τοῦ X Y λέγει: "instead of X he had Y"
  - προσέθετο / προσθήκη: "he added"

## 42.11.4–5: the beginning

The first and second sentences are on pp. 107–108 (Holl's line numbers there are 20–24 and 108, 1). Epiphanius says that from the start Marcion had removed the following, and made the gospel begin with »ἐν τῷ πεντεκαιδεκάτῳ ἔτει Τιβερίου Καίσαρος« καὶ τὰ ἑξῆς:
- everything Luke wrote at the beginning, i.e. »ἐπειδήπερ πολλοὶ ἐπεχείρησαν« καὶ τὰ ἑξῆς (1:1–4);
- τὰ περὶ τῆς Ἐλισάβετ καὶ τοῦ ἀγγέλου εὐαγγελιζομένου Μαρίαν τὴν παρθένον, Ἰωάννου τε καὶ Ζαχαρίου καὶ τῆς ἐν Βηθλεὲμ γεννήσεως (1:5–2:52);
- the γενεαλογία (3:23–38);
- τὰ τῆς τοῦ βαπτίσματος ὑποθέσεως (3:21–22).

Then: ἐντεῦθεν οὖν οὗτος ἄρχεται καὶ οὐ καθ' εἱρμὸν πάλιν ἐπιμένει, ἀλλὰ τὰ μὲν ὡς προεῖπον παρακόπτει, τὰ δὲ προστίθησιν ἄνω κάτω.

Statements of content: 1:1–2:52, 3:21–22 and 3:23–38 are absent, and the text opens with 3:1. Statement of method ("not in sequence; he cuts and adds up and down"): polemic, but it also warns that Epiphanius' copy did not always follow canonical order.

**Holl's source apparatus gives cross-references for these scholia.** On p. 117 it records the Adamantius form of 24:39 (van de Sande Bakhuyzen 198,18ff):

> ἴδετε τὰς χεῖράς μου καὶ τοὺς πόδας μου ὅτι ἐγώ εἰμι αὐτός, ὅτι πνεῦμα ὀστᾶ καὶ σάρκα οὐκ ἔχει καθὼς ἐμὲ θεωρεῖτε ἔχοντα.

This form includes **σάρκα**, after ὀστᾶ. The Adamantius context still has to be checked to see whether it is quoting Marcion's text: see the apparatus at Luke 24:39.
