# Decisions on the doubts

Step 4 of the reading (README §2).

**Status:** proposals only. Nothing in `data/` is changed.

## Standard

The standard is the one set out in `register.md`:
- **Attested absence is absence.** No passage that the witnesses attest absent is proposed.
- **Unattested passages come into question only on a hard inner reason.** A `U?` passage is considered only if the inner mechanics of the certain text require it.
- **The doubts are the Marcion-only wordings.** These are the places where the earlier wording is open.

**History.** An earlier version of this file (`d91d7e9`, `29780c4`) did two things it should not have done:
- it treated attested absences as open questions;
- it proposed passages for seams that hold inwardly: 3:2b–6 and 3:15–17; 19:45–46; 22:54–62; 24:33–35.

Those proposals are withdrawn. `register.md` §1 gives the inner reason each seam holds.

## Passages

**No passage is proposed.** The inner story of the certain text has no gap that requires one.

## Wordings

### T2 — 18:19: "except God alone" (proposed)

| Form | Wording | Witnesses |
|---|---|---|
| Printed (Marcion's text) | εἷς ὁ θεὸς ὁ πατήρ | Epiphanius sch. 50, who reports "the Father" as added against his own copy of Luke; the Dialogue (the Marcionite speaker: "except one, the Father"); Origen and Hippolytus on the heretics' use |
| Canonical (NA27) | εἰ μὴ εἷς ὁ θεός | **Tertullian 4.36.3**, the earliest witness to Marcion's text: "Sed quis optimus, nisi unus, inquit, deus?", without "the Father". Roth allows that the copies differed. |

- **Inner reason.** "God the Father" fixes the relation as a title: a literalization (`register.md` T2). "Except God alone" removes it. Good is the source alone, and "Father" stays relational, as everywhere else in the certain text. It opens no new tension.
- **Witnesses.** Marcion's line is divided. The earliest copy agrees with the canonical wording; the later copies (Epiphanius, c. 375, and the Dialogue) have "the Father".
- **Result:** "except God alone" is proposed for the text before Marcion. The printed form belongs to the later copies of Marcion's text.

### T1 — 10:21: "Lord of heaven" (doubt remains)

| Element | Printed (Marcion's text) | Canonical (NA27) | Witnesses for the printed form |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) address | no vocative πάτερ | πάτερ | Epiphanius sch. 22; Tertullian 4.25.1 |
| (b) "and of earth" | absent | καὶ τῆς γῆς | Epiphanius; Tertullian; **𝔓45** |
| (c) hiding | "what was hidden… you revealed" | "you hid these things… and revealed them" | Tertullian 4.25.1, 6 |

- **Inner reason.** (b) is a weak-to-medium tension, and "and of earth" would remove it. (a) and (c) carry no tension.
- **Witnesses.** 𝔓45, a manuscript of canonical Luke outside Marcion's line, also lacks "and of earth". The short form circulated independently of Marcion's church. A weak inner tension does not decide against two independent lines.
- **Result:** the doubt remains for (a), (b) and (c). The printed form stands.
