# Decisions on the doubts

Step 4 of the reading (README §2), and the doubts registered in `register.md`.

**Status:** proposals only. Nothing in `data/` is changed.

**Order of work:**
1. Part A fixes what each gap requires, from the certain text alone. It is committed before any candidate passage is read.
2. Part B tests the candidates at each position against those requirements and against the witness evidence.
3. Part B records one of four results:
   - supplied;
   - partly supplied;
   - not supplied;
   - more than one candidate fits (the doubt remains).

**Rules (README §1–2):**
- **Continuing is not evidence.** That a passage "continues" or "fits" counts for nothing. Only a requirement stated in Part A counts.
- **The burden is on change.** Nothing in a candidate passage is admitted beyond what the requirement asks for.
- **Witness evidence counts for the text before Marcion:**
  - `A−` (absent from Marcion's text) is a real doubt, not proof of absence;
  - `U−` (silence of a witness who would have used the passage) counts against the passage;
  - `U?` is unknown.

## Part A — Requirements, fixed before the candidates

### R-G1 — The preparing state (gap G1)

From the certain text alone, the step must:

1. **Stand before truth comes down** (4:31). It is the messenger sent "before your face, who will prepare your way before you" (7:27).
2. **Belong to the wilderness.** "What did you go out into the wilderness to look at?" (7:24): the soul goes out to it in a state empty of its structures.
3. **Carry a baptism.** "The baptism of John" (20:4) is something every part at the centre knows.
4. **End the law and the prophets.** "The law and the prophets were until John; since then the kingdom of God is proclaimed" (16:16).
5. **Be more than a prophet and less than the least in the kingdom** (7:26, 28).

The certain text does **not** require any of these:
- a genealogy;
- truth itself going through the preparing state;
- a voice or a naming at that point;
- a testing of truth;
- the end of the preparing state told as an event. 9:7 presupposes John's death, but does not require it to be narrated.

These are not requirements. Material that supplies only them is not admitted on G1.

### R-G4 — Faith's trial (gap G4)

From the certain text alone, the step must:

1. **Fulfil 22:34.** Faith (Peter) denies three times that it knows truth.
2. **Fall on "this day",** after 22:34 and before the herald of morning ("the cock will not crow").
3. **Be faith's own denial of knowing truth** ("that you know me"). It is not a denial of anything else.

Faith turning back afterwards rests on grey alone (22:32 g). It is a lower requirement and is recorded separately.

### R-G2 — The arrival at the centre (gap G2)

From the certain text alone, the step must:

1. **Lie between "he was near Jerusalem" (19:11) and "teaching the people in the temple" (20:1).**
2. **Show truth entering the centre,** and the centre's response to its entering, as at every other arrival (4:31–32; 4:16–30; 19:5–6).

20:8 is certain: "Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things." "These things" are what truth does at the centre. Teaching and proclaiming the good news (20:1) already satisfy that reference, so it adds no further requirement.

### R-G5 — From two powers to all (gap G5)

From the certain text alone, the step must:

1. **Lie between 24:31 (the two recognise truth) and 24:37 (fright and disbelief).**
2. **Say who "they" are at 24:37,** or carry the recognition of the two to the other powers (the eleven, 24:9–11), so that 24:37–41 has a setting.

### R-G3 — The occasion of "these things" (gap G3)

From the certain text alone, the step must:

1. **Lie before 21:7.**
2. **Announce something about the centre** whose time and sign can be asked about. The answer that follows concerns the centre's desolation (21:20) and the shaking of heaven and earth (21:25–26).

### R-T1 and R-T2 — Marcion-only wordings in tension

The candidate is the canonical wording (NA27). It is admitted only if:

1. it removes the tension registered in `register.md` §3;
2. it opens no new tension elsewhere in the certain text;
3. the strength of the witness evidence for Marcion's wording does not outweigh it.

**T1** is 10:21, "Lord of heaven", without "Father" in the address and without "and of earth".

**T2** is 18:19, "No one is good except one, God the Father".

No motive is assigned to any reading (METHOD §2).
