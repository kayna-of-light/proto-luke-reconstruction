# The story of the one soul: read-through

Step 2 of the reading (README §2). This file reads the translation in `reading/` through as one story. It names only movements the text itself repeats. Certainty marks are as in README §1; where a step of the story rests on grey verses alone, the mark is given.

## 1. Movements the text repeats

These are found in the translation. None was assumed before it.

1. **Recognition without reception.** The inner names of truth are spoken first by parts that do not receive it. Each time the speaker is silenced, or itself condemns what it names:
   - the foreign occupants: "the holy one of God" (4:34), "the son of God" (4:41), "son of the Most High God" (8:28);
   - the governing centre: "the Anointed One", "the Son of God" (22:67–71);
   - the outer rule: "the King of the Jews" (23:3).

   Recognition that comes with reception runs a different way. Faith names the inner form and is told to keep silent (9:20–21). The source names it (9:35). Two powers recognise truth when it gives them bread (24:30–31).
2. **The own claims; the lowly receive.**

   | The own | The lowly |
   |---|---|
   | the inherited formation (4:23–29) | Capernaum and the towns |
   | Israel | the centurion (7:9) |
   | the self-righteous host | the strayed woman (7:36–50) |
   | the invited guests | the poor and the lame (14:16–24) |
   | the Pharisee | the tax collector (18:10–14) |
   | the rich ruler (18:18–23) | Zacchaeus (19:1–10) |
   | the nine | the Samaritan (17:11–19) |
   | natural kin | those who hear and do (8:20–21; 11:27–28) |

3. **Holding against letting go.**
   - Parts that hold: the rich fool (12:16–21), the rich man (16:19–31), the ruler (18:23), the servant who hid the mina (19:20–26).
   - Parts that let go: the powers who leave everything (5:11), the sent powers who take nothing (9:3; 10:4), the steward who releases debts (16:1–9), Zacchaeus (19:8).
   - "Whoever wants to save his life will lose it" (9:24).
4. **Outer and inner.** The text names the degrees itself:
   - the cup cleaned outside and full inside (11:39–41);
   - the mouth speaking from the heart (6:45);
   - the tree known by its fruit (6:43–44);
   - "God knows your hearts" (16:15);
   - "the kingdom of God is within you" (17:21).
5. **Rest is for freeing.** On the sabbath truth teaches (4:31) and heals:
   - the withered hand (6:6–10);
   - the bent woman (13:10–16);
   - the following powers eat on the sabbath (6:1–5).

   The last sabbath is kept in rest at the tomb (23:56), and the first day follows (24:1).
6. **Bread taken, blessed, broken, given.** At the feeding (9:16), at the supper (22:19), at Emmaus (24:30). The same act feeds the multitude, gives truth's outer form, and opens the eyes.
7. **The inner form is named when its rejection is told.** Truth calls itself "the son of man" whenever it speaks of being rejected and handed over (9:22; 9:44; 17:25; 22:22; 22:48; 24:7).
8. **Elevation, then descent.** The pattern recurs:
   - the mountain and the choosing, then the level place (6:12–17);
   - the mountain of the transfiguration, then the boy below (9:28–42);
   - the nights on Olivet, then the mornings in the temple (21:37–38);
   - the kneeling on the Mount of Olives, then the handing over (22:41–48).
9. **States closing and opening.**
   - The sun setting before the healings, then day (4:40–42).
   - The night of prayer, then the choosing (6:12–13).
   - The day declining before the feeding (9:12).
   - Day, and the council sits (22:66).
   - Darkness at midday (23:44): the order inverted.
   - Dawn at the tomb (24:1).
   - Evening at Emmaus (24:29 g).
10. **From the periphery to the centre, and away.** The route runs:
    1. Galilee (4:31);
    2. the assemblies of Judea (4:44 g);
    3. a Samaritan village (9:52);
    4. "on the way to Jerusalem", between Samaria and Galilee (17:11);
    5. Jericho (18:35);
    6. "near Jerusalem" (19:11);
    7. the temple (20:1; 21:37–38);
    8. the council, Pilate and Herod in Jerusalem (22–23);
    9. the tomb;
    10. Emmaus, away from Jerusalem (24:13).

## 2. The story

**The soul before.** The soul is ruled from outside. Its time is counted from the emperor, its centre is held by his governor, and its outer degree is divided among partial rulers (3:1 ◊). Nothing in it is shaped from within.

**Truth comes down into the outer degree.** Truth, Divine Truth in outer form, comes down into a settled structure of the soul's outer degree and teaches there in the states of rest (4:31–32). The first thing in the soul to recognise what this truth is inwardly is a foreign occupant in the place of learned teaching. It names the inner form and is silenced and put out, and the soul is not harmed (4:33–35). The soul's inherited formation hears of what truth has done and claims it as its due. When truth does not perform for it, the formation expels truth to the edge of its own foundation, and truth passes through (4:16–30 ◊ order). Every infirmity of the outer degree is brought to truth as a state closes. The foreign parts name the inner form again and are silenced again. The healed parts try to keep truth, and it goes on, because it is sent to every structure (4:40–44).

**Truth gathers its powers.** At truth's word, faith gathers from the soul's depths a fullness its structures cannot hold. It sees its own state, and is turned to gathering the soul's parts into life; the powers leave everything (5:1–11). Truth cleanses what is corrupted and sends it back to the order the soul has received (5:12–14). It names its own inner form for the first time when it releases a paralysed part: "the son of man has authority on earth" (5:24 ◊). It eats with the parts that serve the outer rule. It calls itself physician to the sick and bridegroom to those who are with it. It says that new truth needs new forms (5:27–38). On the days of rest it shows that the inner form is lord of rest, and that rest is for life (6:1–11). After a night of prayer on the mountain it chooses a complete set of powers, with the one that will hand it over among them (6:12–16).

**Truth teaches the whole soul.** On the level place truth speaks to the whole soul, from its centre to its outermost coast (6:17–49):
- the states that claim nothing are where the soul is ruled from within;
- what is full has nothing to receive;
- the soul is to meet what opposes it within as the source does, giving without return;
- what comes forth from the soul shows what it is;
- hearing must go down into doing.

**The soul begins to recognise.** A part in the service of the outer rule knows that truth's word alone acts (7:1–10). A dead offspring is given back to its bereft state (7:11–17). The preparing state asks whether this is what it prepared for, and is answered by what truth does (7:18–28). A strayed part gives everything at truth's feet, while the self-righteous keeping gives nothing (7:36–50). Freed affections provide for truth (8:1–3). The sower and the lamp put everything on the soul's hearing (8:4–18). Truth's kin are those that hear and do (8:20–21). The powers ask "who is this?" when truth calms the storm in the soul's mass of knowing (8:22–25). Across the water a whole army of occupants names the inner form and goes down with the unclean appetites (8:26–39, outcome grey). A bearing part touches truth's outermost edge in faith and is healed (8:43–48). Truth gives its own power to its powers and sends them out with nothing of their own (9:1–6). The partial ruler of the outer degree can explain truth only by what it knows (9:7–9). The little the powers have, turned to the source and given through them, feeds the whole multitude (9:10–17).

**Faith names the inner form, and the source names the son.** Faith names the inner form, "the Anointed One of God", and is told to keep silent. At once truth says that the inner form will be rejected by the soul's governing centre, killed, and raised (9:18–22). The soul's life is kept by losing it (9:23–27). On the mountain truth's outer form shines from within. The law and the prophetic voice appear beside it, and faith would house all three alike. The cloud covers them, and the source itself names the inner form, "my beloved son" (◊). Truth remains alone (9:28–36). Below, the powers cannot free a seized part, and truth names the whole present state "faithless" (9:37–43). The inner form will be handed over into human hands. The greatest is the one that claims nothing (9:43–48).

**The way toward the centre.** Truth turns toward the centre (9:51 g). The region between does not receive it, and the powers who would burn what does not receive are rebuked (9:51–56). The inner form has nowhere to rest in the soul's settled structures, and the dead inheritance is left behind (9:57–62).

Along the way the teaching widens:
- **Sending.** Further powers are sent before truth into every structure. The source is known only through its truth. The interpreting already holds what gives life, and has only to do it (10).
- **Turning to the source.** The soul is taught to turn to the source as Father and ask for its living working. The foreign rule is a strong one overcome by a stronger. The self-righteous keeping cleans the outside and leaves the inside full. The interpreting holds the key and does not enter (11).
- **What the soul holds.** Hypocrisy spreads like leaven. A soul that stores is required in a night. The source feeds and clothes what does not store. The soul waits awake for its lord, who will serve it. Truth's fire divides the soul's household (12).
- **Rest and the shut door.** A daughter of the origin, bound by the foreign rule, is freed on the day of rest. The rule from within grows from the least seed and works hidden through the whole. The door shuts on parts that were near truth only outwardly (13).
- **The feast and the lost.** The source fills its feast with what lacks (14). It goes after the one lost part (15).
- **The soul's holdings.** What the soul holds belongs to another, and serving it cannot go with serving the source. The full state and the empty part are fixed apart by what they loved (16).
- **The kingdom within.** Only the foreign part comes back to give thanks. The kingdom is within, and first the inner form must be rejected by the soul's present state (17).
- **Mercy and letting go.** The source hears what cries to it. The part that strikes its own heart goes home set right. Good is the source alone (18:19 ◊). The ruling part that holds much cannot follow. The blind part at the edge of the way calls truth by an inherited name and is given sight (18). The rich part in the outer service receives truth with joy and lets go (19:1–10). Near the centre, the soul expects the rule at once, and is told of a lord who goes away and returns to see what each power did with what it was given (19:11–26).

**At the centre.** Truth teaches in the soul's centre of worship (20:1). How it came there is not shown (register G2).
- Asked for its authority, truth asks whether the preparing state came from within or from the soul's natural parts. The governing centre cannot answer, because it reasons from fear (20:1–8).
- What bears the outer rule's stamp goes back to the outer rule (20:20–26).
- The part that denies any life beyond the present cannot conceive the risen state (20:27–36).
- The inner form is not the offspring of the soul's past kingship; it is its lord (20:41–44).
- Asked when "these things" will be (register G3), truth describes the soul's upheaval (21). Its powers are handed over and give witness. Its centre is surrounded. Its lights and its inner powers are shaken. Then it sees the inner form come from within with power, and is told to straighten up. Its words outlast heaven and earth.

**The handing over.** At the feast of passing over, a chosen power hands truth to the governing centre for money (22:1–6). Truth gives the powers its outer form as bread and its life as a new covenant (22:14–22). It tells faith that faith will deny it three times this very day (22:31–34). What follows from that is not shown (register G4). On the mount, truth bends low before the source (22:41). The chosen power hands it over with a kiss, and truth names what is handed over: the son of man (22:47–48). Those who hold it cover its eyes and mock its sight (22:63–64). At day, the governing centre asks the inner names. Truth gives the names back to it, and it condemns what it names (22:66–71).

**Before the outer rule.** The governing centre hands truth to the outer rule with charges the story shows to be false (23:2 ◊). The outer rule asks a name of rule, and truth gives it back (23:3). The partial ruler wants a sign and gets no answer (23:7–9). Three times the outer rule finds no evil. The soul's multitude chooses the part that rose against the centre and killed, and truth is delivered "to their will" (23:18–25).

**The death.** Truth is fixed between two parts that broke the order (23:32–33). Darkness covers the soul at midday, its light fails, and the curtain before the inmost is torn (23:44–45). Truth gives its spirit back into the source's hands (23:46). A part of the governing centre that did not consent, and waited for the rule from within, lays the outer form in a state never used before (23:50–53). The affections prepare their anointing and keep the rest (23:55–56).

**The Living One.** At the dawn of the new state, the affections find the outer form gone. The source's messengers name truth "the Living One" and send them to remember what truth said in the outer degree. The powers dismiss it (24:1–11). Two powers walk away from the centre with truth beside them, unrecognised. They know it only by its outer name, and are slow in their will to believe what it told them. At evening, when it takes bread, blesses, breaks and gives, their eyes open. The outer form vanishes once it is recognised (24:13–31). Truth stands among the powers (24:36 g; register G5). It shows hands and feet: it is itself, structured, not an appearance. It asks to receive from them (24:37–43). The turning and release of every part is to be proclaimed in its name to all the nations of the soul (24:47). Here the text ends (register G6).

## 3. The sequence of names

How the names given to the central figure move through the text. "Truth" means Jesus; the inner names describe its inner form.

| Stage | Ref | Name | Speaker | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outer name, from a foreign mouth | 4:34 | "Jesus the Nazarene" | foreign occupant | silenced |
| Inner form, from a foreign mouth | 4:34 | "the holy one of God" | foreign occupant | silenced |
| The own's name | 4:23 | "physician" | inherited formation (voiced by truth) | expulsion |
| Inner form, foreign | 4:41 | "the son of God" / known as "the Anointed One" | foreign occupants | silenced |
| Truth's first self-naming | 5:24 | "the son of man" (authority on earth) | truth | the paralysed part rises |
| Truth takes up the own's name | 5:31 | "physician" (to the sick) | truth | — |
| Relation | 5:34 | "the bridegroom" | truth | its removal foretold |
| Recognised by works | 7:16; 7:22 | "a great prophet"; answered by works | the soul's thoughts; truth | — |
| Question | 8:25 | "Who then is this?" | the following powers | — |
| Inner form, foreign | 8:28 | "son of the Most High God" | the occupation | sent out |
| Outer rule's question | 9:9 g | "Who is this?" | Herod | — |
| Faith names the inner form | 9:20 | "the Anointed One of God" | faith | silence ordered; rejection foretold |
| The source names it | 9:35 ◊ | "my beloved son" | the voice from the cloud | truth found alone |
| Truth names itself and the source | 10:22 ◊ | "the son" / "my Father" | truth | — |
| Inherited name for sight | 18:38–39 | "son of David" | the blind part | the parts in front try to silence it; truth stops |
| Inherited name questioned | 20:41–44 | the Anointed One is David's "Lord", not his son | truth | — |
| Betrayal | 22:48 | "the son of man" | truth | handed over with a kiss |
| The centre speaks the inner names | 22:67–70 | "the Anointed One", "the Son of God" | governing centre | condemnation |
| The outer rule's name | 23:3 | "the King of the Jews" | outer rule | "You say so" |
| After the death | 24:5 | "the Living One" | the source's messengers | dismissed by the powers |
| The outer name again | 24:19 | "Jesus of Nazareth, a prophet mighty in deed and word" | two powers | "slow of heart" |
| Self-naming in glory | 24:26 | "the Anointed One… into his glory" | truth | recognition in the breaking of bread |
| Identity | 24:39 | "I myself" | truth | — |

The sequence moves in four steps:
1. **The inner form is spoken first by what is foreign,** and silenced (4:34 – 8:28).
2. **Truth speaks it itself** (from 5:24), always in connection with authority, rejection or coming.
3. **The soul's own faith speaks it** (9:20), **and the source confirms it** (9:35).
4. **The soul's governing centre and the outer rule speak it and condemn it** (22:67 – 23:3).

After the death it is the source's messengers who give the name ("the Living One"). The powers recognise truth by an act, not by a name (24:31).
