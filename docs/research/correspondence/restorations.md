# Restorations shown by the word-by-word reading

*Compiled from the verdicts in `word_by_word.md`. A passage is listed here only where the reading gave a reason. Every verdict and its reasons are at the place named.*

## Verdicts

Every unprinted passage was tested by its words at its place. Each test gives one of three verdicts:

- **continues** — its words are in the threads of the printed text;
- **break / strain** — its words go against those threads;
- **neutral** — its words are in no thread either way.

*Nearly every unprinted passage continues.* That alone does not put it in the text.

The reading then asks whether the printed text needs the passage. This has two levels:

- **Needed.** The printed text's own words point to the missing passage, or the printed text sets up a movement and never completes it without the passage. Both are given wherever they apply.
- **Leans on.** The printed text opens a strand or a clause that only the passage closes, but the printed line still runs without it.
