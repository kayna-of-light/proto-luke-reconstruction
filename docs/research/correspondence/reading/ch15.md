# Chapter 15 — Luke 17:1–4, 11–12; 4:27; 17:14–35, 37

Marks: `[v]` certain · `[v g]` grey · `[v ◊]` doubtful wording or order (README §1).

## Inner sense

### Stumbling and forgiving [17:1–4]

Causes of stumbling must come, "but woe to the one through whom they come" [17:1]. It would be better for him to be thrown into the sea with a millstone round his neck than to cause one of these little ones to stumble [17:2]. A part of the soul that trips its receptive, unclaiming states would be better sunk in the depths.

"Take heed to yourselves" [17:3]. If your brother sins, rebuke him; if he repents, forgive him. If he sins seven times in a day and turns seven times, forgive him [17:4]. Within the soul, each strayed part is to be rebuked and released as often as it turns. Seven is given as "as often as" (?).

### The ten lepers [17:11; 17:12 ◊; 4:27 ◊ position; 17:14–19]

On the way to Jerusalem, truth passes between Samaria and Galilee [17:11]. It is moving toward the centre, through the region between the outer degree and the region foreign to the inherited worship. Entering a village, it is met by ten lepers [17:12 ◊]: a complete number of corrupted, separated parts.

Here the saying stands [4:27 ◊]: "There were many lepers in Israel in the days of Elisha the prophet, and none of them was cleansed except Neeman the Syrian." Of all the corrupted parts in the inherited people, the one cleansed was the foreign one.

Truth sees them and says: "Go and show yourselves to the priests", and as they go they are cleansed [17:14]. They are sent to the soul's established order, and cleansed on the way. One of them, seeing he is healed, turns back, praising the source with a loud voice [17:15]. He falls on his face at truth's feet, giving thanks, and he is a Samaritan [17:16].

"Were not ten cleansed? Where are the nine?" [17:17]. "Was no one found to return and give glory to God except this foreigner?" [17:18]. "Rise and go; your faith has saved you" [17:19]. Nine parts go on to the established order. The one from outside the inherited worship turns back to truth itself. Its faith saves it, and the saying about Elisha is fulfilled in front of the reader.

### The kingdom within [17:20–21 ◊]

The self-righteous keeping asks when the kingdom of God will come [17:20]. "The kingdom of God is not coming with observation; nor will they say, 'Look, here it is!' or 'Look, there it is!' For behold, the kingdom of God is within you" [17:21 ◊]. The rule from within does not come as something observed outside. It is within.

### The days of the son of man [17:22; 17:23–24 g; 17:25–26; 17:27 g; 17:28; 17:29–31 g; 17:32; 17:33–35 g; 17:37 g]

"Days are coming when you will long to see one of the days of the son of man, and you will not see it" [17:22]. A state will come in which the soul longs for the inner form and cannot see it. Grey: do not follow those who say "there" or "here"; like lightning across the whole sky, so will the son of man be in its day [17:23–24 g]. "But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation" [17:25]. Before the inner form appears, it is rejected by the soul's present state.

As in the days of Noah, so in the days of the son of man [17:26]. Grey: they ate and drank and married until the flood came [17:27 g]. As in the days of Lot: "they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting and building" [17:28]. The soul goes on taking in and building up its ordinary life until the day it is overturned.

Grey [17:29–31 g]:
- fire and sulphur rain on Sodom on the day Lot goes out;
- so on the day the son of man is revealed;
- the one on the housetop is not to come down for his goods, and the one in the field is not to turn back.

"Remember Lot's wife" [17:32]: the one who turned back toward what was being left (cf. 9:62).

Grey [17:33–35 g; 17:37 g]:
- whoever seeks to keep his life will lose it;
- of two in one bed, one is taken; of two women grinding, one is taken;
- "Where, Lord?" "Where the body is, there the eagles will gather."
