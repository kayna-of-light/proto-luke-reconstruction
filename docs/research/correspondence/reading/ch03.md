# Chapter 3 — Luke 6:12–49

Marks: `[v]` certain · `[v g]` grey · `[v ◊]` doubtful wording (README §1).

## Inner sense

### The mountain and the choosing [6:12–14; 6:15 g; 6:16]

Truth goes out to the mountain to pray and spends the night "in the prayer of God" [6:12]. It is lifted toward the source and remains in communion with it through a dark state. "When day came", as the new state opens, it calls its following powers and chooses twelve of them, a complete set, and names them emissaries: powers that are sent [6:13].

The first named is Simon, "whom he also named Peter": faith, given a new name by truth [6:14]. The others follow: Andrew, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew; grey, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus and Simon the zealot [6:15 g]; Judas son of James. The individual functions of these names cannot be derived from the text (?). Last is "Judas Iscariot, who became a traitor" [6:16]. From the moment the soul's complete set of sent powers is chosen, a power that will hand truth over is among them.

### The level place [6:17; 6:18 g; 6:19 ◊]

Truth comes down with them from the elevation and stands on a level place [6:17]. A great crowd of its following powers is there, with a great multitude from all Judea and Jerusalem and from the coast of Tyre and Sidon. The whole soul meets truth here: its centre, and its outermost coast that lies beyond the inherited land. Grey: they come to hear and to be healed, and those troubled by unclean spirits are healed [6:18 g]. The whole multitude tries to touch truth, because power goes out from it and heals all [6:19].

### Blessed and woe [6:20 ◊; 6:21–26]

Truth lifts its eyes to its following powers and speaks.

- **The poor** are blessed: "theirs is the kingdom of God" [6:20 ◊]. The states that claim nothing as their own are where the soul is ruled from within.
- **The hungry and the weeping** are blessed [6:21]. The states of lack now are the states that will receive: they will be filled, they will laugh.
- **The hated and excluded** are blessed when the soul's other parts hate the following parts, exclude them and cast out their name as evil "on account of the son of man" [6:22]. They are rejected because of the inner form. Their reward is great "in heaven", in the inner degree. The soul's inheritance always did this to the voice that calls it back: "that is what their fathers did to the prophets" [6:23].
- **Woe to the rich**, the states that hold much as their own: they have already received their consolation [6:24].
- **Woe to the full and the laughing**: those full now will hunger, and those laughing now will mourn [6:25].
- **Woe when all speak well of you**: that is how the inheritance treated the false voice [6:26].

What is full has nothing left to receive.

### Love of enemies [6:27–31; 6:32–33 g; 6:34–35; 6:36–38 ◊]

Truth says to those who are listening how the soul is to meet what opposes it within itself:
- love what hates it, do good to it, bless and pray for what curses and mistreats it [6:27–28];
- offer the other cheek, and give the tunic as well when the cloak is taken [6:29];
- give to everything that asks, and do not demand back what is taken [6:30];
- do to others what it wishes done to itself [6:31].

Love that answers only love is exchange: even the strayed parts love what loves them [6:32–33 g] and lend to receive back as much [6:34]. Love, do good and lend expecting nothing [6:35]. Then "you will be sons of the Most High, for he is kind to the ungrateful and the wicked". The soul's parts become offspring of the source when they act as the source acts. "Be merciful, as your Father is merciful" [6:36 ◊]: the Father is Divine Love, giving where nothing is returned.

Do not judge, do not condemn, pardon [6:37 ◊]. Give, and a full measure, pressed down and running over, is put into your lap; with the measure the soul gives out, it receives back [6:38 ◊].

### Blind guides, the beam, the tree [6:39–43; 6:44 g; 6:45–46]

A closed understanding cannot lead a closed understanding: both fall into the pit [6:39]. The learning power is not above the teacher, but fully trained it becomes like the teacher [6:40]: the following powers are to become like truth.

The soul sees the speck in its brother's eye, a small blockage of sight in another of its parts, but does not see the beam in its own eye, the greater blockage of its own sight [6:41]. It wants to correct the other part while its own sight is blocked. "Hypocrite": remove the beam first, then you will see clearly [6:42].

No good tree bears bad fruit, and no bad tree good fruit [6:43]. Each tree is known by its fruit: figs are not gathered from thorns [6:44 g]. What comes forth from the soul shows what the soul is. The text states the degrees itself: the good person brings forth good from the good treasure of the heart, "for the mouth speaks out of the abundance of the heart" [6:45]. The outer form (the mouth) speaks from the will (the heart).

"Why do you call me 'Lord, Lord', and do not do what I say?" [6:46]. Naming truth with the inner name, without doing, is recognition in words only.

### The two houses [6:47–49 g]

Grey. The part that comes, hears and does is like a man who digs deep and lays the foundation on rock. When the river bursts against the house, it cannot shake it. The part that hears and does not do builds on the ground without a foundation, and the house collapses. A soul's state of life stands or falls by whether hearing goes down into doing.
