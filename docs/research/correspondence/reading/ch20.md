# Chapter 20 — Luke 22:1–23, 31–34, 39–42, 45–48, 63–71

Marks: `[v]` certain · `[v g]` grey · `[v ◊]` doubtful wording (README §1).

## Inner sense

### The feast draws near; the handing over is arranged [22:1; 22:2 g; 22:3–4 ◊; 22:5; 22:6 g]

The feast of Unleavened Bread draws near, "which is called the Passover" [22:1]. It is the feast of passing over, eaten with bread that has no leaven, and leaven was the pretence of the self-righteous keeping (12:1). Grey: the chief priests and scribes seek how to put truth to death, for they fear the people [22:2 g].

Judas called Iscariot, "who was of the number of the twelve" [22:3 ◊], goes and confers with the chief priests and officers how he might hand truth over to them [22:4 ◊]. A power among the chosen goes to the soul's governing centre to deliver truth into its hands. In the printed text no foreign power is said to enter it; the handing over comes from the power itself (◊). The governing centre is glad and agrees to give it money [22:5]: it hands truth over for something the soul can hold as its own. Grey: it looks for an opportunity, away from the multitude [22:6 g].

### Preparing the Passover [22:7 g; 22:8; 22:9–13 g]

Grey: the day comes on which the Passover lamb must be sacrificed [22:7 g]. Truth sends faith and John to prepare the Passover, "that we may eat it" [22:8]. Grey [22:9–13 g]:
- in the city they will meet a man carrying a jar of water, and are to follow him into the house he enters;
- they are to say to the master of the house, "The Teacher says…";
- he will show them a large upper room, already furnished;
- they find it as truth said.

### The bread and the cup [22:14–15; 22:17–18 g; 22:19–20; 22:21 g; 22:22; 22:23 g]

When the hour comes, truth reclines with the emissaries, the sent powers [22:14]. "I have earnestly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer" [22:15]. Grey: it takes a cup, gives thanks, and gives it to be divided, saying it will not drink of the vine "until the kingdom of God comes" [22:17–18 g].

Truth takes bread, gives thanks, breaks it, and gives it to them: "This is my body, which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me" [22:19]. Truth in outer form gives itself as the good that sustains the soul. The act is the one at the feeding (9:16): taken, blessed, broken, given. Here what is given is truth's own outer form. "Do this in remembrance": the soul's repeated taking-in keeps truth present in it.

Likewise the cup after the meal: "This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you" [22:20]. A new binding between the source and the soul, made in truth's life, which is poured out.

Grey: the hand of the one who betrays is on the table [22:21 g]. "For the son of man goes as it has been determined, but woe to that man by whom he is betrayed!" [22:22]. The inner form goes where it must. Woe to the power through which it is handed over. Grey: they ask one another which of them it could be [22:23 g].

### Faith's readiness and the denial foretold [22:31–32 g; 22:33–34]

Grey [22:31–32 g]:
- "Simon, Simon, look, Satan has demanded to have you, that he might sift you like wheat";
- "I have prayed for you that your faith may not fail. And when you have turned again, strengthen your brothers".

The foreign rule asks to shake faith apart like grain on a sieve. Truth prays that faith does not fail, and that once it has turned back it will strengthen the other powers.

Faith says: "Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death" [22:33]. Truth answers: "I tell you, Peter, the cock will not crow this day until you deny three times that you know me" [22:34]. Faith declares itself ready for anything. Truth tells it that before the herald of morning sounds, on this same day, it will deny three times that it knows truth.

### The mount [22:39–40 g; 22:41; 22:42 g; 22:45–46 g]

Grey: truth goes out, as is its custom, to the Mount of Olives, its powers following, and tells them to pray that they may not enter into temptation [22:39–40 g].

Truth withdraws from them about a stone's throw, kneels down and prays [22:41]. At the elevation, truth goes a little apart from its powers and bends low before the source.

Grey: "Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done" [22:42 g]. Truth in outer form gives up its own will to the source's will.

Grey: rising, truth finds its powers sleeping "for sorrow": "Why are you sleeping? Rise and pray that you may not enter into temptation" [22:45–46 g].

### The kiss [22:47–48]

While truth is still speaking a crowd comes, led by Judas, one of the twelve, who draws near to kiss truth [22:47]. "Judas, would you betray the son of man with a kiss?" [22:48]. The power among the chosen leads the soul's multitude and hands truth over with the sign of love. Truth names what is being handed over: the inner form.

### Mocked and blindfolded [22:63–64; 22:65 g]

The men holding truth in custody mock it and beat it [22:63]. They blindfold it and ask, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?" [22:64]. The parts of the soul that hold truth strike it, cover its eyes, and demand that it see. They mock its sight. Grey: they say many other things against it, blaspheming [22:65 g].

### The council [22:66–67; 22:68 g; 22:69–71]

"When day came", the assembly of the elders, the chief priests and the scribes gathers and leads truth to their council [22:66]. At the opening of a new state, the soul's governing centre sits in judgement on truth. "If you are the Anointed One, tell us." Truth answers: "If I tell you, you will not believe" [22:67]. Grey: "and if I ask, you will not answer" [22:68 g].

"But from now on the son of man shall be seated at the right hand of the power of God" [22:69]. From this point, the inner form is joined to the source's power to act.

"Are you the Son of God, then?" "You say that I am" [22:70]. The governing centre speaks the inner name itself, the name the foreign occupants spoke (4:41). Truth gives the name back to the speaker. "What further testimony do we need? We have heard it ourselves from his own lips" [22:71]. The centre hears the name and condemns what it names. It recognises without receiving, as the foreign parts did.
