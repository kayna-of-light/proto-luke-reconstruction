# Chapter 8 — Luke 10:1–11, 16–17, 19–28

Marks: `[v]` certain · `[v g]` grey · `[v ◊]` doubtful wording (README §1).

## Inner sense

### The seventy sent ahead [10:1 ◊; 10:2–3 g; 10:4–5; 10:6 g; 10:7–11]

"The Lord" appoints seventy others and sends them ahead, two by two, into every town and place where it is about to come [10:1 ◊]. Truth sends further powers before it into every structure of the soul that it will enter. Why they go in pairs cannot be derived beyond the pairing itself (?). Grey: the harvest is plentiful and the workers few; they go "like lambs among wolves" [10:2–3 g].

They carry no purse, no bag and no sandals, and greet no one on the road [10:4]. They have nothing of their own, and do not attach to anything on the way. On entering a house they first say "Peace to this house" [10:5]. Grey: if a son of peace is there, the peace rests on him; if not, it returns [10:6 g].

- **Where they are received,** they stay in that house and eat and drink what it provides, without moving from house to house [10:7]. Where a town receives them, they eat what is set before them, heal its sick and say, "The kingdom of God has come near to you" [10:8–9]. Where a state of the soul receives the powers, they take what that state gives, heal it, and declare the rule from within near.
- **Where they are not received,** they wipe the town's dust off against it: "Yet know this: the kingdom of God has come near" [10:10–11]. Where they are not received, the rule from within has come near all the same.

### The chain of receiving [10:16]

Whoever listens to the powers listens to truth, and whoever rejects them rejects truth. Whoever rejects truth rejects "the one who sent me" [10:16]. The chain runs from the powers, through truth, to the source.

### The return [10:17 g; 10:19; 10:20 g]

Grey: the seventy return with joy: "Lord, even the daimons submit to us in your name!" [10:17 g]. Truth has given them authority "to tread on snakes and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy; and nothing will harm you" [10:19]. Snakes and scorpions strike from the ground, at the feet, with poison: what strikes the soul at its outermost (?). The powers walk over it unharmed. Grey: they are to rejoice not that the spirits submit, but that their names are written in the heavens [10:20 g].

### Truth rejoices: Father and son [10:21–22 ◊]

In that same hour truth rejoices "in the holy spirit", in the living working of the source [10:21 ◊]. It says: "I thank you, Lord of heaven, that what was hidden from the wise and understanding you have revealed to little children. Yes, Father, for so it was pleasing in your sight." What the soul's own wisdom and understanding could not see, the source shows to its states that claim nothing.

"All things have been handed over to me by my Father; and no one knows who the Father is except the son, and who the son is except the Father, and anyone to whom the son chooses to reveal him" [10:22 ◊]. Truth calls the source "my Father" and itself "the son". The source is known in the soul only through its own truth. What truth is inwardly is known only to the source, and is shown to the soul by truth itself.

### Blessed eyes [10:23–24]

Truth turns to its following powers, privately: blessed are the eyes that see what they see [10:23]. Many prophets and kings wanted to see and hear it and did not [10:24]. What the soul's prophetic voice and its kingship longed for, the soul's understanding now sees.

### The lawyer's question [10:25 ◊; 10:26–28]

A part of the soul's interpreting stands up to test truth: "Teacher, what must I do to inherit life?" [10:25 ◊]. Truth sends it back to what it holds: "What is written in the law? How do you read it?" [10:26]. It answers [10:27]:
- "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your strength, and with all your mind": love of the source with every degree, from the will (heart) to the understanding (mind);
- "and your neighbour as yourself".

"You have answered rightly; do this, and you will live" [10:28]. The interpreting part already holds what gives life. Doing it is life.
