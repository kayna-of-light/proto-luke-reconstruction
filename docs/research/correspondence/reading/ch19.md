# Chapter 19 — Luke 21:7–17, 19–20, 23–38

Marks: `[v]` certain · `[v g]` grey · `[v ◊]` doubtful wording (README §1).

## Inner sense

### The question [21:7]

"Teacher, when will these things be, and what will be the sign when these things are about to take place?" [21:7]. The soul, at its centre, asks when "these things" will come, and by what sign. The printed text does not say what "these things" are. The answer goes on to describe them.

### Not led astray [21:8–9]

"See that you are not led astray. For many will come in my name, saying, 'I am he!' and, 'The time is at hand!' Do not go after them" [21:8]. Many parts of the soul will use truth's name, claim to be truth, and announce the moment. The soul is not to follow them. When it hears of wars and tumults it is not to be terrified: these must come first, "but the end will not be at once" [21:9].

### Upheaval [21:10–11]

Nation rises against nation and kingdom against kingdom [21:10]: the soul's peoples and ruling principles turn against one another. There will be earthquakes, famines and pestilences, terrors and great signs from heaven [21:11]. The soul's ground shakes, its sustenance fails, sickness spreads, and signs appear in its inner degree.

### Before all this: witness [21:12–17, 19]

Before all this, the following powers will be seized and persecuted [21:12]. They are handed over to the assemblies and to prison, and brought before kings and governors "for my name's sake". The soul's teaching places, its rulers and the outer rule all take hold of what follows truth. "This will be your opportunity to bear witness" [21:13].

They are not to prepare their defence beforehand [21:14]. "I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which none of your adversaries will be able to withstand or contradict" [21:15]. Truth itself gives them speech.

They are handed over even by parents, brothers, relatives and friends, and some are put to death [21:16]: the soul's natural kin betray what follows truth (cf. 12:53). They are hated by all for truth's name [21:17]. "By your endurance you will gain your lives" [21:19].

### The centre surrounded [21:20; 21:23–24 g]

"When you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation has come near" [21:20]. When the soul's centre is ringed by the forces of the outer rule, its desolation is near.

Grey [21:23–24 g]:
- alas for those who are pregnant or nursing;
- there will be great distress and wrath;
- they will fall by the sword and be led captive;
- Jerusalem will be trampled by the nations until their times are fulfilled.

### Signs and the coming of the son of man [21:25–26; 21:27 ◊; 21:28]

Signs appear in sun, moon and stars [21:25]: the soul's lights, which order its times. On earth, in the outer degree, the nations are in distress and perplexity at the roaring of the sea and the waves: at the surging of the soul's mass of knowing. People faint with fear and foreboding, "for the powers of the heavens will be shaken" [21:26]. Even the inner degree's powers, as the soul has known them, are shaken.

"And then they will see the son of man coming from the heavens with great power" [21:27 ◊]. Then the soul sees the inner form coming from the inner degree, with power. "When these things begin to take place, straighten up and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near" [21:28]. The soul is told to do what the bent woman could not (13:11 g): to straighten up and look up.

### The fig tree [21:29–33]

Look at the fig tree and all the trees: when they come into leaf, summer is near [21:29–30]. When the soul sees these things, the kingdom of God is near [21:31]. The outward signs show the inner season. "This generation will not pass away until all has taken place" [21:32]. "Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away" [21:33]. The inner and outer degrees, as they now stand, pass. Truth's words do not.

### Keeping the heart light [21:34–35; 21:36 g]

The soul is to watch itself, "lest your hearts be weighed down with dissipation and drunkenness and cares of this life" [21:34]. A will that is weighed down is caught when that day comes on it suddenly, "like a trap", which comes on all who dwell on the face of the whole earth [21:35]. Grey: stay awake and pray, so as to stand before the son of man [21:36 g].

### Day in the temple, night on the mount [21:37–38]

Every day truth teaches in the temple, and at night it goes out and lodges on the mount called Olivet [21:37]. By day it teaches at the soul's centre of worship. At night it withdraws to an elevation outside the centre. All the people come early in the morning to hear it in the temple [21:38]: at the opening of each new state, the soul's multitude comes to the centre to hear.
